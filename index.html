    The Weekly Rhythm at 26 Sapele St

    WEEKDAY BUCKETS TO SUNDAY CARVERY

    The lapa lights come on as the evening does. Every day of the week brings its own crowd to Elspark.

    Mondays · 09:00–19:00

    Range Stays Open

    While the pub and kitchen take a breather on Mondays, the Elspark Golf Driving Range stays open for after-work practice.

    Tuesdays · from 18:30

    Beer Pong or Quiz Night

    Beer pong or a quiz at the bar, with selected pizzas at half price. Range open until 22:00 for a bucket first.

    Wed Poker · Thu Bingo & Karaoke

    Poker, Bingo & Karaoke

    Poker on Wednesdays and bingo with karaoke on Thursdays, both from 18:30. Two red-felt pool tables every night.

    Match Days

    Big-Screen Live Rugby

    Watch the Springboks and Emirates Lions on screen with fellow locals, cold draughts, and hot pizzas from the kitchen.

    Sundays · 09:00–18:00

    Sunday Carvery & Live Music

    Traditional Sunday family carvery praised for great value, paired with live afternoon entertainment and kids on the jungle gym.

    Birthdays & Groups

    Covered Function Marquee

    Spacious tented patio area right alongside the fenced lawn playground — message us on WhatsApp with your date and guest count.
